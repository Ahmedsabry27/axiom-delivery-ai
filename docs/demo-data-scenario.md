# Enterprise transformation demonstration scenario

`Axiom Demo Enterprise` is a fictional, GBP-denominated FY27 Q1 workspace. Customer Experience Modernisation is amber due to UAT pressure, Core Platform Resilience is red because identity integration is overdue, and Operations Automation is green with measurable benefits.

The central narrative is `DEP-017 — Identity API contract and token exchange`. It blocks `IDAM-241`, threatens the Identity Integration Complete milestone and Atlas 3.2, and is paired with `RISK-008`. Existing health, attention, readiness, sprint, and portfolio services calculate downstream effects from these persisted inputs.

The seed includes fourteen fictional personas spanning executive, portfolio, programme, project, delivery, release, finance, approval, governance, team-member, and restricted-viewer roles. Development authentication supplies their claims; the seed never creates passwords.

Current core counts are 1 portfolio, 3 programmes, 8 projects, 6 teams, 9 sprints, 18 work items, 5 releases, 8 milestones, 3 dependencies, 5 RAID items, 28 evidence records, 5 outcomes, 15 investment snapshots, 8 meetings, and 6 safe integration configurations.
