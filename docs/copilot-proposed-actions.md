# Copilot proposed actions

AX-EP03 supports internal `DRAFT`, `PROPOSED`, and `PENDING_APPROVAL` records only. The review drawer shows the exact action, editable content, target, evidence context, risk classification, expected review boundary, and approval requirement.

Saving or submitting does not send messages, create meetings, modify Jira, change external RAID records, or call external tools. `APPROVED`, `EXECUTED`, and `FAILED` are reserved for the AX-EP07 approval/execution lifecycle.
