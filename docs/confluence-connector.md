# Confluence Cloud connector

The Confluence simulator synchronizes three spaces and 24 versioned page source records, including fingerprints, classifications, authorized source references, mappings to knowledge/evidence, and quality reporting. Source content remains authoritative and is never rendered as executable HTML.

Live Confluence v2 pagination, permission mirroring, sanitization of real storage-format content, evidence-repository writes, attachment metadata, optimistic page updates, and sandbox validation remain deferred. Outbound proposals are approval-required and the simulator never reports a provider write as successful.
