# Teams Meetings connector

The simulator contains eight meetings, six transcripts, twelve extracted review items, one absent transcript, and a permission/admin-setting warning. Transcript absence is represented as unavailable, never as empty content. Recording binaries, chats, and channel history are out of scope.

The connector is deliberately `DEGRADED` when transcript access is not fully evidenced. Live transcript notification timing, admin-consent detection, transcript retrieval, restricted evidence persistence, Meeting Intelligence handoff, subscription renewal, and approved follow-up execution remain pending sandbox validation.
