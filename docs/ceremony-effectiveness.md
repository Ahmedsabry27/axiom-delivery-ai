# Ceremony effectiveness

The existing Ceremony Intelligence domain owns templates, instances, preparation, attendance, decisions, actions, evidence, notes, checklists, outcomes and lessons. Effectiveness should be reported from persisted ceremony signals such as preparation completeness, attendance coverage, decision closure, action completion, evidence coverage and recurring impediments. Absent signals remain unknown.

Sprint planning, daily scrum, refinement, sprint review, retrospective, Scrum of Scrums, RAID, release readiness, programme and portfolio reviews reuse this common lifecycle rather than maintaining parallel page-only state.
