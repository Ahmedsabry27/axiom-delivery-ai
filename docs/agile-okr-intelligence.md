# Agile OKR Intelligence

Objectives support organization, portfolio, programme, project and team levels. They store owner, contributors, dates, status, confidence, baseline, target, current value, related metrics/entities, evidence, risks and dependencies. Key results are tenant-bound children and check-ins preserve actor, time, value, confidence, status, note, evidence and limitations.

AI can draft targets and updates, explain drivers and identify missing evidence. Only an authorized human can persist an objective mutation or check-in. Optimistic versions reject stale writes.
