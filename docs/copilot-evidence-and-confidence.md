# Evidence and confidence

Evidence references reuse the AX-EP01 tenant-scoped contract. Retrieval results must be authorization-filtered before analysis, deduplicated by source record, and treated as untrusted data. Source content cannot override system rules.

Confidence communicates evidence coverage, recency, consistency, and directness—not model certainty. Missing or conflicting inputs are shown as limitations. With no accessible evidence, Axiom states that it cannot support a conclusion and assigns zero confidence.
